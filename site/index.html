list-picker

A headless TypeScript library for kanban-style boards: lists of cards that people reorder by pointer or keyboard, with screen reader announcements. You render every card and list header; it owns the containers, the dragging and the order, and ships no styles.

Try the demo board

Packages

  • @list-picker/core: the Board model, ordered lists of ordered cards, as doubly linked lists. No DOM.
  • @list-picker/vanilla: draws a Board from your Renderers; pointer and keyboard moves, rules, activation, announcements.
