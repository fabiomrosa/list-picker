The lists' behaviour.
Your lists' look.

list-picker is a headless library of sortable lists for TypeScript. It owns the containers, the moves and the order of lists people reorder: a kanban board with many lists, a playlist with one. Dragging by pointer or keyboard, screen reader announcements, a linked-list model. You render every item and list header, and it ships no styles.

Install The demos

Same board, your look:

Drag a card or a list title. Or focus a card, press Space, move it with the arrows and press Space again. Doing holds 3 cards.

Packages

PackageWhat it is
@list-picker/core
The Board model: ordered lists of ordered cards, events, snapshots. No DOM.
@list-picker/vanilla
Draws a Board from your Renderers; pointer and keyboard moves, rules, activation, announcements.
@list-picker/reactReact hooks over the same core and engine: the Board as state, the engine on your own elements.
@list-picker/angularThe same board as Angular components. Page coming soon.
