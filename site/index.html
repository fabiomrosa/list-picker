list-picker

A headless TypeScript library for kanban-style boards: lists of cards that people reorder. You render every card and list header; list-picker owns the containers, the moves and the order, and ships no styles.

  • The model is a Board of doubly linked lists with an id index: a position is "before this card", never an index.
  • Cards and lists move by pointer (mouse, pen, touch) and by keyboard.
  • Every pick up, move, drop and cancel is announced to screen readers.
