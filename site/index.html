headless sortable lists for TypeScript

The lists' behaviour.
Your lists' look.

list-picker owns the containers, the moves and the order of lists people reorder: a kanban board with many lists, a playlist with one. Dragging by pointer or keyboard, screen reader announcements, a linked-list model. You render every item and list header, and it ships no styles.

Same board, your look:

Drag a card or a list title. Or focus a card, press Space, move it with the arrows and press Space again. Doing holds 3 cards.
